01 • Retail Employee

🛍️ What Is Retail?

Retail employees help stores serve customers and keep products ready to sell.

  • Customer service and sales floor work.
  • Cashier and checkout responsibilities.
  • Stocking, inventory and online pickup.
  • Cleaning, recovery and presentation.
02 • Retail Employee

🏬 Common Entry Roles

🧾

Cashier

Checkout, payment procedures and customer interaction.

📦

Stocker

Unload, organize, label and replenish merchandise.

🙋

Sales Associate

Help customers locate items and maintain the sales floor.

03 • Retail Employee

⏰ A Typical Shift

Clock in and get your assignment.
Check your area and priority tasks.
Help customers while completing assigned work.
Recover, stock, clean and communicate with the team.
Finish closing/hand-off tasks before leaving.
04 • Retail Employee

🙋 Customer Service Basics

👋

Greet

Acknowledge customers and offer help.

👂

Listen

Understand what the customer is asking for.

🧭

Help

Give accurate directions or find a coworker who can assist.

😌

Stay Professional

Do not argue; follow store procedures when a situation needs a supervisor.

05 • Retail Employee

💳 Cash Handling & Checkout

Cashiers must follow the employer’s exact procedures.

  • Scan items accurately and pay attention to the screen.
  • Handle cash/cards according to policy.
  • Count change carefully when required.
  • Protect customer payment information.
  • Call for help with overrides, suspicious transactions or unfamiliar situations.
06 • Retail Employee

📦 Stocking & Inventory

📦

Receive

Move merchandise safely according to store procedures.

🏷️

Label

Match items, locations and pricing/signage.

🧱

Stock

Place products neatly and safely.

🔎

Check

Notice damaged, missing or misplaced merchandise and report it.

07 • Retail Employee

📵 Phones & Professionalism

Retail work happens in front of customers.

  • Keep personal phone use within workplace rules.
  • Wear required uniform/name badge.
  • Use respectful language.
  • Avoid discussing private customer or employee information.
08 • Retail Employee

💬 Handling a Difficult Customer

Stay calm and listen.
Do not promise something outside your authority.
Explain the next step or store policy respectfully.
Get a supervisor when needed.
