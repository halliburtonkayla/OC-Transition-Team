Aim on the lake. Cast. When the bobber dips, tap HOOK IT! Hold REEL, then release when the tension turns red. Catch five fish.

How to fish

  1. Drag your finger across the lake to choose where the bobber lands. Tap CAST LINE.
  2. Watch the bobber. When you see and hear a bite, tap HOOK IT! quickly.
  3. Hold HOLD TO REEL to bring the fish toward shore. Release when the tension bar nears red, then reel again. A snapped line lets the fish escape.
  4. Catch five fish to complete the lake challenge. Each catch shows its species and weight.

On a keyboard: Space casts or hooks; hold Space to reel. Sound starts after you interact. Vibration works on devices and browsers that support it.
