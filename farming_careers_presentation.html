02 • Farming & Agriculture

🌽 Crop Farming

Crop work changes throughout the year.

  • Prepare soil or fields.
  • Plant and monitor crops.
  • Manage weeds, pests and irrigation according to the farm plan.
  • Harvest and handle crops safely.
  • Maintain records and equipment.
03 • Farming & Agriculture

🐄 Livestock Farming

Animal care requires consistent routines.

  • Feed and water animals.
  • Observe behavior and health.
  • Clean and maintain facilities.
  • Assist with movement, breeding or care according to training.
  • Follow biosecurity and animal-handling procedures.

06 • Farming & Agriculture

🦺 Farm Safety

Agriculture has serious hazards, so training matters.

  • Stay clear of moving machinery and power take-off areas.
  • Use protective equipment required for the task.
  • Follow chemical labels/training and employer procedures.
  • Use safe animal-handling methods.
  • Know emergency plans for heat, storms and injuries.

08 • Farming & Agriculture

🎓 Training & Education

There are many ways to learn agriculture.

  • High-school agriculture/CTE and FFA experiences.
  • On-the-job training and farm experience.
  • Technical or college programs in agriculture, mechanics, agribusiness or related fields.
  • Specialized licenses/certifications may be required for certain tasks.
