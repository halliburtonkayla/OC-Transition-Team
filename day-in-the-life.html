Student Information



1. What did this worker actually do?

Describe at least three duties or responsibilities you noticed. Be specific.

2. What was the work environment really like?

Think about where the person worked, noise/activity level, indoors/outdoors, working alone or with others, and the pace of the day.

3. Tools, technology & equipment

What did the worker use to do the job? Which tool or technology would you need to learn?

4. Skills in action

Choose two skills you actually saw the worker use. Explain what happened that showed each skill.

5. The challenging part

What looked hardest, most stressful, or most demanding about this job? How do you think you would handle that part?

6. Training reality check

Before watching, what training did you think this career required? Now compare that with the training pathway shown above. Would you be willing to complete it? Why or why not?

7. Something that changed my thinking

What did you learn that you did not know before—or what was different from what you expected?

8. Could I actually see myself doing this?

Rate each statement: 1 = Not at all, 5 = Definitely.
