HVAC Careers

Heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and refrigeration keep buildings comfortable and systems operating.

2 / 15
🌡️

What HVAC Techs Do

Install, maintain, inspect, and troubleshoot heating/cooling equipment and related controls and airflow systems.

3 / 15
🏠

Where They Work

Homes • schools • stores • hospitals • offices • factories • refrigeration and service companies.

4 / 15
⚙️

Systems Thinking

A technician considers temperature, airflow, controls, electrical components, mechanical components, and system condition together.

5 / 15
🧰

Maintenance

Filters, coils, drains, belts or other components depending on equipment, cleanliness, inspections, and manufacturer procedures can affect system performance.

6 / 15
🦺

Safety

Technicians follow electrical, refrigerant, ladder, tool, ventilation, and other job-specific safety requirements and work within their training.

7 / 15
🎓

Training

Career/technical programs, trade schools, community colleges, apprenticeships, and employer training are common pathways. Some work requires specific certifications/licenses.

8 / 15
🔧

Tools

Meters • gauges • thermometers • hand tools • leak-detection/service tools • diagnostic equipment, depending on role and qualifications.

9 / 15
🗣️

Customer Communication

Explain what you observed, what testing shows, available next steps, and what you cannot confirm yet—without making up an answer.

10 / 15
💬

Workplace Scenario

A customer says one room is always hot. Ask questions, inspect systematically, gather measurements, and follow diagnostic procedures instead of immediately replacing parts.

11 / 15
☀️

Work Conditions

HVAC may involve attics, rooftops, crawl spaces, weather, driving to service calls, and busy seasons.

12 / 15
📈

Moving Up

Experience and additional credentials can lead to advanced service, controls, refrigeration, estimating, sales, supervision, or business ownership.

13 / 15
🤔

Is This Career for You?

Do you like fixing systems, using tools and technology, traveling to different work locations, and solving practical problems?

14 / 15
✅

Career Check

What does HVAC stand for? Why are measurements better than guessing during troubleshooting?
