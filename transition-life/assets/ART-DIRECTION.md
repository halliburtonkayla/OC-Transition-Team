# Coordinated scene set

Created with the image-generation tool for this repository. The existing `transition-town-master.png` informed the polished illustrated game direction; no existing map or business artwork was replaced. The store exterior was the visual reference for the interior scenes, and the empty-home scene was the reference for the other home rooms. The original exterior PNG remains as a future reference; runtime scenes use compressed WebP.

Shared prompt direction: polished welcoming illustrated 3D town-game environment; warm daylight and warm overhead lighting; deep teal and ivory brand colors; walnut/oak furniture and floors; brass accents; matching HOME & BEYOND house-and-spark emblem and signage; coordinated camera and architecture; no stock photography, blocky voxel/Minecraft/Roblox appearance, unrelated brand styling or baked-in software controls. Gameplay controls, exact prices, inventory, scan states and room placements are live HTML/SVG overlays.
