04 • Gaming Careers

🧪 What Game Testers Actually Do

Testing is more than “getting paid to play.”

  • Follow test plans and repeat actions carefully.
  • Find and reproduce bugs.
  • Write clear reports with steps and evidence.
  • Retest after fixes.
  • Stay focused even when repeating the same section many times.

06 • Gaming Careers

📹 Streaming Is a Business

Successful content requires work away from the controller.

  • Choose a content idea and schedule.
  • Record or stream with usable audio/video.
  • Edit clips, titles and thumbnails.
  • Follow platform/community rules.
  • Study what viewers respond to without giving up safety or privacy.

08 • Gaming Careers

🎓 Training Paths

The best preparation depends on the job you want.

  • Coding/programming for development.
  • Art/design portfolios for creative roles.
  • Testing/documentation practice for QA.
  • Video editing, speaking and branding for content roles.
  • Business, marketing or event skills for esports operations.
09 • Gaming Careers

🗂️ Build a Portfolio

Make a small game, level, mod, art sample or edited video.
Finish it—small completed work is useful evidence of skill.
Organize your best work in a simple portfolio.
Ask for feedback and improve it.
