Landscaping Careers

Create and maintain outdoor spaces at homes, businesses, parks, schools, and other properties.

2/15
🎯

Career Options

Grounds worker, landscape crew member, mower/operator, irrigation worker, horticulture worker, crew leader, designer, and business owner.

3/15
⭐

Daily Tasks

Mowing, trimming, planting, mulching, cleanup, watering, pruning, bed maintenance, and other seasonal work.

4/15
📚

Plants & Soil

Successful landscaping uses knowledge of plant needs, sunlight, soil, water, climate, spacing, and seasonal care.

5/15
💡

Equipment

Mowers, trimmers, blowers, hand tools, wheelbarrows, trailers, irrigation tools, and other equipment used only with proper training.

6/15
🛠️

Safety

PPE, hydration, heat/cold precautions, traffic awareness, lifting, equipment guards, chemical rules, and safe tool use.

7/15
🎯

Planning a Job

Understand the customer's request, inspect the site, estimate materials/time, organize the crew, complete work, and clean up.

8/15
⭐

Customer Service

Clarify expectations before starting, protect property, communicate changes, and avoid promising work beyond the agreed job.

9/15
📚

Workplace Scenario

You find an object hidden in tall grass before mowing. Stop, remove/report the hazard safely, inspect the area, then continue according to procedure.

10/15
💡

Weather

Outdoor workers plan around heat, cold, rain, storms, ground conditions, and seasonal workloads.

11/15
🛠️

Professional Skills

Reliability, physical stamina, attention to detail, teamwork, equipment care, time management, and communication.

12/15
🎯

Training

Many roles offer on-the-job training. Horticulture, equipment, pesticide/applicator, irrigation, or landscape education/certifications may apply to specialized work.

13/15
⭐

Moving Up

Crew member → skilled specialist/operator → crew leader → supervisor/designer/estimator → business ownership depending on skills and requirements.

14/15
📚

Career Check

Name five landscaping tasks and three safety concerns. Why should a crew inspect an area before using equipment?

15/15
💡

Try It

Design a simple school flower bed on paper: choose a shape, measure it, select pretend plants, make a materials list, and plan maintenance.
