Career Options

Cosmetologist, hairstylist, barber, nail technician, esthetician, makeup artist, salon assistant, manager, educator, and owner.

Hair Services

Depending on training and license: consultation, shampooing, cutting, styling, chemical services, texture services, and hair/scalp care.

📚

Barbering

Barbers may specialize in cuts, fades, clipper work, facial-hair grooming, styling, and client consultation within their licensed scope.

5/15
💡

Client Consultation

Ask what the client wants, clarify expectations, consider service limitations, communicate cost/time, and avoid promising impossible results.

6/15
🛠️

Sanitation

Cleaning, disinfection, hand hygiene, safe product use, tool handling, and following board/workplace sanitation requirements protect clients and workers.

7/15
🎯

Training & Licensing

Education, exams, hours, and licensing differ by state and profession. Students should verify current requirements with their state licensing authority.

8/15
⭐

Tools

Combs, brushes, clippers, shears, dryers, styling tools, capes, sanitation supplies, and role-specific products/equipment.

9/15
📚

People Skills

Listening, conversation, boundaries, professionalism, scheduling, handling complaints, and making clients feel welcome.

10/15
💡

Workplace Scenario

A client shows a photo of a style that may not work exactly with their hair. Explain respectfully what is realistic and discuss alternatives.

11/15
🛠️

Business Side

Appointments, inventory, pricing, retail products, tips, records, marketing, expenses, taxes, and customer retention can all matter.

12/15
🎯

Build a Client Base

Consistent service, professionalism, cleanliness, communication, referrals, and responsible marketing help build repeat clients.

13/15
⭐

Moving Up

Professionals may specialize, become educators/managers, rent/work in different salon models, or pursue ownership subject to laws and licensing.
