CNA & Nursing Careers

Explore jobs that help patients feel safe, comfortable, and cared for.

2 / 15
🛏️

What Does a CNA Do?

CNAs help with daily care such as bathing, dressing, meals, mobility, comfort, and reporting changes to the nursing team.

3 / 15
👩‍⚕️

What Does a Nurse Do?

Nurses assess patients, give treatments and medicines as allowed by their role, document care, teach patients, and work with the healthcare team.

4 / 15
🏥

Where Could You Work?

Hospitals • nursing homes • assisted living • home health • clinics • rehabilitation centers.

5 / 15
⏰

A Day on the Job

Expect teamwork, walking, communicating with patients, following care plans, documenting work, and responding when someone needs help.

6 / 15
💙

Skills That Matter

Compassion • communication • dependability • observation • teamwork • privacy • staying calm.

7 / 15
🧤

Safety & Infection Control

Handwashing, gloves and other protective equipment, safe lifting, clean work areas, and following facility procedures are essential.

8 / 15
🎓

Training Path

CNA training can be a faster entry into healthcare. Nursing paths may include LPN/LVN, RN, and additional education for advanced roles. Requirements vary by state.

9 / 15
🩻

Tools & Technology

Blood-pressure equipment • thermometers • mobility equipment • electronic health records • call systems.

10 / 15
💬

Workplace Scenario

A patient says they feel dizzy when standing. What should you do? Stay with them, help keep them safe, and promptly report the change according to workplace procedures.

11 / 15
🤝

Professional Behavior

Protect patient privacy, arrive on time, use respectful language, follow directions, and ask for help when a task is outside your training.

12 / 15
📈

Moving Up

A healthcare worker may build experience and continue education toward additional certifications, nursing roles, specialties, leadership, or other health careers.

13 / 15
🤔

Is This Career for You?

Do you like helping people? Can you stay respectful during personal-care tasks? Are you comfortable working as part of a team?

Career Check

Name two places a CNA or nurse might work. What is one safety skill? What is one professional skill?
