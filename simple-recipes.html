Dump. Mix. Cook. Build Confidence.

Start with easy recipes that use a short ingredient list and clear steps. Choose a category below, open a recipe, and use it for planning, digital practice, or supervised hands-on cooking.

CDC/Visual adaptation: reduce each recipe to 1–3 steps at a time, use picture/ingredient matching, and offer “First → Then → Done” prompts.

Kitchen safety: Students should use the level of adult supervision appropriate to their skills. Follow appliance instructions, use oven mitts, avoid cross-contamination, and use a food thermometer for meat/poultry. The website activities are educational practice, not a substitute for hands-on safety instruction.
