Entrepreneurship & Small Business

A business starts with solving a real problem for a customer—not just picking a cool name.

2/15
🎯

What Is an Entrepreneur?

Someone who organizes resources to offer a product or service and accepts responsibility for decisions, costs, customers, and risk.

3/15
⭐

Business Ideas

Lawn care, cleaning, childcare where legally permitted, crafts, food businesses subject to rules, photography, beauty services with licensing, tutoring, online services, and more.

4/15
📚

Who Is the Customer?

Define who needs the product/service, what problem it solves, where customers are, and why they would choose your business.

5/15
💡

Costs

Supplies, equipment, transportation, fees, insurance, advertising, labor, technology, taxes, and other expenses affect what a business actually earns.

6/15
🛠️

Pricing

A price should consider costs, time/labor, market conditions, value, and the need to sustain the business—not just what sounds cheap.

7/15
🎯

Money In vs Profit

Revenue is money the business receives. Profit is what remains after business expenses. High sales do not automatically mean high profit.

8/15
⭐

Customer Service

Respond professionally, set expectations, show up, communicate delays, correct problems appropriately, and protect your reputation.

9/15
📚

Marketing

A clear name/message, photos or examples, referrals, social media where appropriate, flyers, local networking, and good service can help customers find you.

10/15
💡

Workplace Scenario

A customer asks for extra work that was not included in the agreed price. Clarify the request and price/time change before doing additional work.

11/15
🛠️

Legal & Safety Basics

Businesses may need registrations, licenses, permits, insurance, contracts, tax records, age-specific requirements, or professional credentials depending on location and activity.

12/15
🎯

Keep Records

Track income, expenses, receipts, appointments, inventory, customer agreements, and other records needed to understand the business.

13/15
⭐

Growing the Business

Improve systems, retain customers, raise capacity carefully, add services strategically, and avoid growing faster than quality and finances can support.

14/15
📚

Career Check

What is the difference between revenue and profit? Name five possible business expenses and three ways to earn repeat customers.

15/15
💡

Build a Business

Choose a simple business idea. Write the customer, service/product, three startup costs, a sample price, how customers find you, and one rule/safety issue to research.
