01 • Welding & Pipe Welding

🔥 Welding vs. Pipe Welding

🔥

Welder

Joins and repairs metal using welding processes, drawings, measurements and safety procedures.

🧯

Pipe Welder

Specializes in joining pipe systems where fit-up, position, quality and leak-free welds matter.

03 • Welding & Pipe Welding

🧑‍🏭 What the Work Looks Like

A welding shift can involve planning, preparation and hands-on metalwork.

  • Read measurements, drawings or weld symbols.
  • Prepare and clean metal surfaces.
  • Fit pieces together and tack them in place.
  • Make welds and inspect the finished work.
  • Keep the work area safe and organized.
04 • Welding & Pipe Welding

🧰 Tools & Equipment

🥽

PPE

Helmet, gloves, protective clothing, safety glasses and hearing protection as required.

⚙️

Shop Tools

Welding machine, grinder, clamps, measuring tools and material-handling equipment.

📐

Measurement

Tape, square, levels and layout tools help keep work accurate.

🧹

Prep & Cleanup

Good welds begin with preparation and end with inspection and cleanup.

05 • Welding & Pipe Welding

🛢️ Pipe Welding Skills

Pipe work adds another layer of precision.

  • Fit pipe and joints correctly before welding.
  • Work in different welding positions.
  • Control heat and technique around the pipe.
  • Follow procedures and quality requirements.
  • Understand that some jobs require testing or certifications.
06 • Welding & Pipe Welding

🦺 Safety Is Part of the Job

👀

Eyes & Face

Protect against arc light, sparks and flying particles.

🌬️

Ventilation

Fumes and gases require proper ventilation and job-site controls.

🔥

Fire Prevention

Hot work can create fire hazards; follow the site’s hot-work rules.

⚡

Equipment Safety

Inspect equipment and follow electrical and machine-safety procedures.

07 • Welding & Pipe Welding

🎓 Training Path

People enter welding through several routes.

  • High-school CTE or dual-enrollment opportunities.
  • Technical school or community-college welding programs.
  • Apprenticeship or employer training.
  • Practice toward employer-required welding tests or credentials.
Training requirements vary by employer and the type of welding work.
08 • Welding & Pipe Welding

⏱️ A Day on the Job

Review the assignment and safety requirements.
Gather materials, PPE and tools.
Measure, prep and fit the metal or pipe.
Weld according to the procedure.
Inspect, clean and document/repair if required.
