COACH KAYLA’S QUICK GUIDE

Let’s play softball.

  1. Playing a friend? Tap Create Two-Player Game. Your friend opens this same game on their device, enters your room code, and taps Join. Keep both screens open.
  2. Batting: Watch the pitch. Tap SWING as the ball reaches home plate. The gold timing zone helps. Move the pad left/right before the pitch to aim. Contact swings are lower; power swings fly higher.
  3. Pitching: Choose Fast, Changeup, or Drop. Move the pad to aim at the white strike zone, then tap PITCH. Four balls award a walk; three strikes are an out.
  4. Fielding: After a hit, the camera pulls back. Drag the pad toward the gold landing ring. You control the player with the blue ring. Catch or pick up the ball, then tap a base to throw. Tap Closest fielder to switch.
  5. Running: Runners go one base automatically. Tap Run another base to try for more, or Hold next base to stop at the next bag. A catch sends runners back.

Three outs switch sides. Most runs wins; ties go to extra innings. This is an accessible exhibition game: no stealing, bunts, tag-ups, or infield-fly rule.

Computer: WASD or arrow keys move/aim · Space swings or pitches · 1–4 throw · Q switches fielders · E advances · H holds.

iPhone: Turn sideways for a wider field. You can still scroll to reach every control in either orientation.
