Culinary & Restaurant Careers

There is much more to a restaurant than the person cooking the food.

2 / 15
🍽️

Restaurant Team

Host • server • cashier • dishwasher • prep cook • line cook • baker • manager • catering staff.

3 / 15
🧾

Front of House

Greeting guests, taking orders, answering questions, handling payments, serving food, and creating a welcoming experience.

4 / 15
🍳

Back of House

Preparing ingredients, cooking to standards, keeping stations stocked, communicating orders, cleaning, and working quickly as a team.

5 / 15
🧼

Food Safety

Clean hands, preventing cross-contamination, correct storage, safe temperatures, clean equipment, and following local/workplace food-safety rules.

6 / 15
🎫

Reading Orders

Accuracy matters. Staff must notice modifications, quantities, table/order numbers, allergies communicated through proper procedures, and timing.

7 / 15
🙂

Customer Service

Listen, stay polite, repeat important details, correct mistakes through workplace procedures, and involve a supervisor when appropriate.

8 / 15
⏱️

Busy Rush

During a rush: communicate, prioritize, keep the station organized, help teammates, and do not sacrifice safety for speed.

9 / 15
🎓

Training

Many restaurant roles can be learned on the job. Culinary programs and food-safety credentials can support advancement depending on the role.

10 / 15
🥄

Tools

POS register • kitchen equipment • thermometers • knives • measuring tools • cleaning/sanitation supplies.

11 / 15
💬

Workplace Scenario

The wrong side dish reaches a table. Listen, apologize professionally, follow the restaurant's correction process, and communicate with the kitchen.

12 / 15
📈

Moving Up

Crew member → trainer/lead → shift supervisor → manager, or kitchen pathways toward specialized cooking, pastry, catering, or chef roles.

13 / 15
🤔

Is This Career for You?

Do you enjoy teamwork, food, people, movement, and a workplace where priorities can change quickly?

14 / 15
✅

Career Check

Sort these jobs: host, line cook, server, dishwasher, cashier. Which are front of house, back of house, or could vary?

15 / 15
🎯

Restaurant Challenge

Assign a team as host, cashier/server, cook, and customer. Take a mock order, prepare a pretend meal, deliver it to the correct customer, and handle one surprise request.
