01 • Content Creator

📱 What Is a Content Creator?

A content creator plans and produces media for an audience.

  • Short-form or long-form video.
  • Photos, graphics and written posts.
  • Podcasts or livestreams.
  • Educational, entertainment, lifestyle, gaming, beauty, food or other niches.
02 • Content Creator

🎬 The Work Behind the Post

Choose an idea and audience.
Plan the hook, shots or talking points.
Record or create the content.
Edit, caption, title and publish.
Review performance and improve the next piece.

05 • Content Creator

🔒 Safety & Privacy

Creators should protect themselves and other people.

  • Avoid revealing private information such as home, school or real-time location unnecessarily.
  • Get appropriate permission before featuring other people.
  • Use strong account security.
  • Think before posting something permanent.
06 • Content Creator

©️ Copyright & Original Work

Publishing online does not mean everything online is free to reuse.

  • Create original material when possible.
  • Use music, images and clips you have permission or a license to use.
  • Understand platform rules for copyrighted content.
  • Credit alone does not automatically give permission.
