WELCOME TO THE SHOW

Ready, Set, Work!

  1. Choose solo practice or 2–4 teams sharing one screen and keyboard.
  2. Pick a category and point tile.
  3. Read the question. Press Space or tap a buzzer to buzz in. In teams, Q, P, Z, and M are the team buzzers. Space belongs to the team shown on screen.
  4. After buzzing, choose an answer or press its letter key (A, B, or C); number keys also work. Learn from the feedback, then return to the board.

Each category has a 100-point and a 200-point question. Solo players can try a question twice; a correct second try earns half points. Teams get one attempt each, so another team can buzz after a miss. Scores stay in this game.

Choose two-choice support or an untimed game during setup. Read aloud and Pause are available during questions. This version uses one shared keyboard for teams; separate-device buzzers are not connected.

Teacher notes & sources

These are practice scenarios about workplace expectations. Results show recorded game responses, not a certification or teacher observation. Group responses are team results; they are not assigned to individual student reports.
