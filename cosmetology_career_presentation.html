01 • The Industry

What Is Cosmetology?

Cosmetology is the professional study and practice of beauty services. Depending on training and licensing, professionals may work with hair, nails, skin, makeup, grooming, or specialized services.

💇🏽‍♀️

Hair

Cutting, styling, coloring, chemical services, natural hair care and extensions.

💅🏽

Nails

Manicures, pedicures, nail art, enhancements and nail health.

✨

Skin

Facials, skincare routines, hair removal and other services within the professional's license.

✂️
02 • Hair Careers

Cosmetologist & Hair Stylist

Consult. Ask what the client wants and discuss realistic options.
Create. Shampoo, cut, style, color or perform permitted chemical services.
Protect. Follow sanitation, product-safety and infection-control procedures.
Build clientele. Rebooking, customer service and professional communication matter.
🪮 ✂️ 🧴
🎨 💇🏽‍♀️ 🪞

Possible workplaces: salons, spas, specialty studios, resorts, entertainment settings, or self-employment.

03 • Nail Careers

Nail Technician

💅🏽

Services

Manicures, pedicures, polish, nail art and enhancement services allowed by the technician's training and license.

🧼

Sanitation

Clean tools, safe work practices and preventing cross-contamination are essential professional responsibilities.

🎨

Creative Skills

Color theory, detail work, steady hands, design and following client inspiration.

📅

Business Skills

Appointments, time management, customer service, inventory and repeat clients.

04 • Skincare Careers

Esthetician

Estheticians focus on professional skincare and beauty services that fall within their scope of practice.

🫧

Facials & Skin Care

Client consultation, cleansing, skincare services and product education.

🌿

Spa Services

Services can include exfoliation and hair-removal techniques when permitted by state rules and training.

🔎

Know Your Scope

An esthetician is not automatically a medical provider. State law determines which services each license may perform.

Professional habit: Never diagnose a medical skin condition outside your role. Refer clients appropriately when something needs medical evaluation.

06 • Education & Licensing

From Student to Professional

Explore the career. Compare cosmetology, barbering, nails and esthetics before choosing a program.
Choose an approved school/program. Ask about tuition, supplies, schedules, completion requirements and job placement support.
Complete required training. Requirements differ by license and state.
Meet licensing requirements. This may include applications, exams, fees and other state requirements.
Keep learning. Trends change, but sanitation, professionalism and client safety stay essential.
TENNESSEE

Students should verify current requirements directly with the Tennessee Board of Cosmetology and Barber Examiners before enrolling or applying for a license.

Requirements can change; this presentation is career-exploration guidance, not a licensing application.

07 • Workplace Readiness

What Makes a Great Beauty Professional?

⏰

Reliability

Arrive on time, manage appointments and communicate when plans change.

🤝

Customer Service

Listen carefully, stay respectful and handle concerns professionally.

🧼

Cleanliness

Follow sanitation procedures every time—even when the salon is busy.

🗣️

Communication

Ask questions before beginning a service and explain aftercare clearly.

Think about it: In beauty careers, your technical skill may bring a client in—but professionalism helps bring the client back.
💵
08 • The Business Side

Your Talent + Business Skills

💵

Income Can Vary

Pay can depend on job type, location, experience, hours, clientele, tips, commission and self-employment expenses.

📱

Marketing

A professional portfolio, referrals and responsible social media can help people discover your work.

🧾

Expenses

Independent professionals may need to plan for products, tools, space, taxes, insurance and other business costs.

Career question: Would you rather work for an established salon, rent/work independently where permitted, or eventually own a business? Each path has different responsibilities.
