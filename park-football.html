School directory & game rules

School names, mascots and listed colors identify the teams. Jerseys are game illustrations; player numbers are fictional and all schools have equal abilities. No student rosters are used.

Quick-play rules: four 90-second quarters, four downs for ten yards, punts, field goals, interceptions and safeties. Touchdowns include an automatic extra point. Drives after a score start at the 25; kickoffs and most penalties are omitted. Ties go to sudden-death overtime. One person controls each team, with computer teammates.

COACH KAYLA’S PLAYBOOK

Let’s play football.

  1. Find “YOU.” The mint ring marks the player you control. Drag the circle to move; hold SPRINT with your other finger.
  2. On offense: choose a play and tap SNAP. Run with the quarterback, HAND OFF to the running back, or tap A, B, or C to pass. You automatically control the receiver after a catch.
  3. Move the chains. Reach the gold line within four downs. Keep going into the end zone for a touchdown. Pass before crossing the blue line.
  4. On defense: chase the ball carrier. Tap SWITCH to select the closest defender, then TACKLE when you’re close.
  5. Play online: both people pick a school. One creates a room and shares the code or invite. The other joins. The creator taps START MATCH once both teams are there.

Keyboard: arrows / WASD move · Shift sprint · Space snap/tackle · 1/2/3 pass · H handoff · E switch · J juke · K kick.

Four short quarters. Automatic extra points. Touch controls support moving and pressing an action together.
