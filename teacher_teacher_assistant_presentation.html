Teacher & Teacher Assistant

Education careers help students learn skills, build confidence, solve problems, and prepare for their futures.

2/15
🎯

Teacher Duties

Plan lessons, teach, assess learning, manage the classroom, communicate with families/staff, document progress, and support students.

3/15
⭐

Teacher Assistant Duties

Support instruction, help individuals/small groups, prepare materials, supervise as assigned, reinforce routines, and work under teacher/school direction.

4/15
📚

Where They Work

Public/private schools, preschool/early childhood programs, special education settings, career/technical programs, and other learning environments.

5/15
💡

More Than Teaching

Educators organize materials, attend meetings, communicate, complete documentation, plan, supervise, and solve unexpected problems.

6/15
🛠️

Classroom Management

Clear expectations, consistent routines, respectful redirection, active supervision, and following school procedures help learning happen.

7/15
🎯

Different Learners

Students learn in different ways. Educators use approved supports, accommodations, visuals, examples, practice, and collaboration.

8/15
⭐

Training Paths

Teacher licensure and education requirements vary by state and teaching area. Assistant/paraprofessional requirements also vary by employer and program.

9/15
📚

Technology

Learning platforms, presentation tools, communication systems, student information systems, assistive technology, and classroom devices.

10/15
💡

Workplace Scenario

A student says, 'I don't understand.' Instead of simply giving the answer, break the task down, ask what part is confusing, and provide appropriate support.

11/15
🛠️

Professional Boundaries

Protect student privacy, follow reporting/safety procedures, communicate professionally, and maintain appropriate adult-student boundaries.

12/15
🎯

Professional Skills

Patience, communication, organization, adaptability, creativity, teamwork, and reliability.

13/15
⭐

Is It for You?

Do you enjoy explaining things, helping young people, organizing, speaking to groups, and adjusting when a plan changes?

14/15
📚

Career Check

How do teacher and assistant roles differ? Name three responsibilities people may not think of as 'teaching.'

15/15
💡

Try It

Teach a partner a simple skill in three steps. Ask a check-for-understanding question and change your explanation if they are confused.
