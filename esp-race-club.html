WELCOME TO THE CLUB

A race everyone can reach.

Steer with ← →. Hold ↑ to speed up. Hold ↓ to slow down. Collect stars and steer around cones. One lap finishes the race, and every racer gets to finish.

Cruise assist keeps your car moving, so one finger or a mouse is enough to play. Turn it off to practise holding ↑ while steering.

Choose your screen

Big classroom board: two side-by-side views or four views in a grid. All racer buttons stay along the bottom. Your board must support multiple simultaneous touches for children to play together. Try the buttons with two people before class.

Phone or small screen: one big view. Two or four racers take turns on the same course. Turn times are compared after everyone finishes. Small screens always use this layout.

Keyboard controls

  • Coral: arrow keys
  • Blue: W / A / S / D
  • Gold: I / J / K / L
  • Violet: numpad 8 / 4 / 5 / 6

In turn mode, arrow keys control the current racer. Escape pauses. Keyboard hardware may limit how many keys can be held together.

iPhone: if full screen is unavailable, use Safari’s Share → Add to Home Screen → Open as Web App, then launch from your Home Screen. You can also play in the browser.
