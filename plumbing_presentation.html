Plumbing Careers

Plumbers install, maintain, troubleshoot, and repair systems that carry water, waste, gas, and other materials depending on the job.

2/15
🎯

What Plumbers Do

Read plans, measure and cut materials, install fixtures and piping, test systems, find leaks, repair problems, and follow codes.

3/15
⭐

Where They Work

Homes, schools, businesses, hospitals, factories, construction sites, and service companies.

4/15
📚

Tools of the Trade

Pipe wrenches, cutters, measuring tools, levels, inspection tools, hand tools, and job-specific equipment.

5/15
💡

Measure Twice

Accurate measurements, fitting selection, slope, layout, and careful planning help prevent expensive mistakes.

6/15
🛠️

Safety First

PPE, tool safety, confined-space rules where applicable, sanitation, lifting safety, and following jobsite procedures matter.

7/15
🎯

Training Path

Apprenticeships, career/technical programs, trade schools, community colleges, and employer training are common paths. Licensing rules vary.

8/15
⭐

Service Calls

A plumber may listen to a customer's problem, inspect the system, diagnose the cause, explain options, complete approved work, and verify the repair.

9/15
📚

Construction Plumbing

New construction requires coordination with other trades and following plans, schedules, inspections, and applicable codes.

10/15
💡

Workplace Scenario

A customer says a sink keeps leaking. Ask questions, inspect systematically, identify the source, and avoid replacing parts just by guessing.

11/15
🛠️

Professional Skills

Dependability, problem-solving, customer service, math, communication, cleanliness, and attention to detail.

12/15
🎯

Moving Up

Experience can lead to advanced trade work, foreperson roles, estimating, inspection, project management, or business ownership where requirements are met.

13/15
⭐

Is It for You?

Do you like fixing practical problems, working with tools, moving between jobsites, and seeing a finished result?

14/15
📚

Career Check

Name three places plumbers work, three useful skills, and two reasons accurate measurements matter.

15/15
💡

Try It

Use classroom-safe materials to plan a pretend pipe route between two points. Measure the route, choose fittings, and explain your plan.
