Firefighter & EMT Careers

Explore emergency-service careers built around teamwork, preparation, service, and safety.

2 / 15
🔥

Firefighter Duties

Fire suppression, rescue, emergency response, equipment checks, training, station duties, prevention/community education, and other responsibilities depending on department.

3 / 15
🚑

EMT Duties

EMTs provide emergency medical care within their training and scope, assess patients, communicate with dispatch/medical teams, document care, and transport when appropriate.

4 / 15
🤝

Teamwork

Emergency scenes rely on defined roles, communication, training, incident procedures, and following leadership directions.

5 / 15
🎓

Training Never Stops

Emergency workers practice skills, review procedures, maintain equipment, and continue required education because skills must be ready before an emergency occurs.

6 / 15
💪

Physical Demands

Some roles involve lifting, carrying equipment, stairs, heat/cold, protective gear, long shifts, interrupted sleep, and stressful situations.

7 / 15
🦺

Safety

Scene assessment, protective equipment, infection control, traffic awareness, accountability, and working within training are essential.

8 / 15
📻

Communication

Workers gather information, speak calmly, relay accurate updates, document facts, and communicate with patients, families, coworkers, and other agencies.

9 / 15
📚

Training Path

Requirements vary by state and department. Fire academy/training, EMT education/certification, testing, background/medical/physical requirements, and continuing education may apply.

10 / 15
🧰

Equipment

Protective gear • radios • medical equipment • rescue tools • vehicles • hoses and other department-specific equipment.

11 / 15
💬

Workplace Scenario

You arrive where people are upset and information is unclear. Follow scene-safety procedures, communicate calmly, gather facts, and work within your assigned role and training.

12 / 15
⭐

Professional Qualities

Dependability • courage with judgment • compassion • fitness • teamwork • emotional control • willingness to train.

13 / 15
🤔

Is This Career for You?

Can you follow procedures under pressure, work closely with a team, keep learning, and help people on difficult days?
