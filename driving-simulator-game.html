Controls & route map

Touch: drag the wheel left/right; hold the pedals (press lower on the accelerator for more power). Hold a mirror or Look behind to observe.

Keyboard: arrows steer/drive/brake. P/R/N/D select gears. B seatbelt, Enter engine, Q/E signals. Hold 1/2/3 for left/rear/right mirrors, 4 look behind. Space pauses.

Start facing the training building. Back toward Town Avenue and steer to line up with the right-hand lane. The small map shows your position and the destination bay.
