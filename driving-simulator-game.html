TRANSITION TOWN · DRIVER TRAINING

Let’s learn the controls.

You start parked, facing the school. Your destination is the Grocery Store. Choose Practice with hints for instructions while you drive.

On your phone or iPad

  1. Turn your phone sideways. This gives you more room to see the road.
  2. Tap Seatbelt. Hold each mirror and Look behind briefly to check the area.
  3. Keep one finger holding BRAKE. With another finger, tap Start, then R for Reverse. A quick tap on BRAKE won’t keep it held.
  4. Let go of BRAKE. Hold the top of GAS gently to back up slowly. Release GAS and hold BRAKE to stop.
  5. Drag the wheel left or right to steer. You can hold GAS with one finger and steer with another. Letting go of the wheel straightens it.
  6. Stop and hold BRAKE before changing gears. Tap D to drive forward, or P to park.

You can tap How to drive anytime. The lesson pauses while you read. Practice hints are optional; the car always responds to your controls.

HOW TO DRIVE · READ AT YOUR OWN PACE

You control the car.

Tap switches like Seatbelt, Start, gears and signals. Hold the pedals and mirror buttons. Drag the steering wheel sideways.

1. Get ready

Tap Seatbelt. Hold the rearview, left and right mirrors for about half a second each. Hold Look behind to check behind the car.

Keep a finger holding BRAKE. With another finger, tap Start, then R. On a computer, hold ↓, press Enter, then R.

2. Back out

Let go of BRAKE. Hold near the top of GAS for gentle power. Press lower on the pedal for more power. Keep backing speed slow.

Begin with the wheel straight. Back away from the school until the yellow arrow on the little map is near the road. Then drag the wheel LEFT while reversing so the front of the car turns up the road.

Release GAS and hold BRAKE to stop. Keep BRAKE held while you tap D. Check both side mirrors before entering the road.

3. Drive the route

Let go of BRAKE and gently hold GAS. Drag the wheel sideways to steer. The number on the dashboard is your speed in MPH; this route’s limit is 15.

Stay in the right-hand lane. Release GAS early and hold BRAKE to stop before the white stop line. Wait for the pedestrian to clear the crosswalk and check both directions.

Tap the ▶ right signal. Move into the intersection and steer RIGHT onto the cross street. Follow the yellow dotted route on the map toward Grocery.

4. Park at Grocery

Signal right again and turn into the Grocery Store parking lot. Point the car toward the store—the yellow map arrow should point down.

Use gentle GAS and small steering movements to line up in the green parking bay. Hold BRAKE until the speed is 0, then tap P. The report opens once your car is properly inside the bay.

If P does not finish the drive, you’re outside the bay or facing the wrong direction. Hold BRAKE, select D or R, and adjust your position.

What the gears mean

P — Park: secures the stopped car. R — Reverse: moves backward. N — Neutral: no drive power. D — Drive: moves forward.

Computer controls

↑ hold to accelerate · ↓ hold to brake · ← / → hold to steer · B seatbelt · Enter start engine · P/R/N/D gears · Q/E left/right signals · hold 1/2/3 left/rear/right mirrors · hold 4 look behind · Space pause.

Hiding hints keeps the drive going. Opening this guide pauses your car and traffic. Reading the guide does not count as a mirror check or driving action.
