01 • Childcare Worker

🧸 What Does a Childcare Worker Do?

Childcare workers help children stay safe, cared for, engaged and learning.

  • Supervise children and maintain safe routines.
  • Lead age-appropriate play and learning activities.
  • Help with meals, cleanup, hygiene and transitions.
  • Communicate with families and coworkers.
  • Observe children and follow center policies.

04 • Childcare Worker

⏰ A Typical Day

Prepare the room and greet children.
Supervise play, centers and learning activities.
Manage snacks/meals, bathroom or diaper routines.
Help children solve problems and regulate emotions.
Clean, document and communicate at pickup.
05 • Childcare Worker

🛡️ Safety Comes First

Childcare workers must follow their program’s safety rules.

  • Maintain active supervision.
  • Know emergency procedures and authorized pickup rules.
  • Keep choking hazards, chemicals and unsafe items away from children.
  • Follow sanitation, food and health procedures.
  • Report concerns according to workplace requirements.

07 • Childcare Worker

🎓 Training & Requirements

Requirements depend on the employer, setting and state rules.

  • Employers may require background checks and health/safety training.
  • CPR/first aid may be required or strongly preferred.
  • Some roles require specific education or early-childhood credentials.
  • Experience with children can help when applying.
08 • Childcare Worker

💬 Handling Big Feelings

😢

Stay Calm

Use a steady voice and keep everyone safe.

🧩

Find the Need

Is the child tired, hungry, frustrated or needing help communicating?

↪️

Redirect

Offer safe choices and guide the child toward the next activity.

📝

Communicate

Follow center procedures for documenting or sharing important events.

09 • Childcare Worker

🔐 Professional Boundaries

Caring is important—and so is professionalism.

  • Protect children’s privacy.
  • Follow workplace rules for photos, social media and communication.
  • Do not share confidential family information.
  • Use appropriate language and behavior at all times.
