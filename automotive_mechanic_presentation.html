Automotive & Mechanic Careers

Turn an interest in cars, tools, and problem-solving into career possibilities.

2 / 15
🚗

What Technicians Do

Inspect vehicles, diagnose problems, perform maintenance, repair systems, test work, and explain findings.

3 / 15
🛞

Career Options

Automotive technician • tire/lube technician • diesel technician • collision repair • parts specialist • service advisor.

4 / 15
🧰

Start With Maintenance

Oil and filter service, tires, batteries, brakes, fluids, lights, and inspections are common areas for developing foundational skills.

5 / 15
💻

Diagnostics

Modern technicians combine hands-on inspection with scan tools, service information, measurements, and logical troubleshooting.

6 / 15
🥽

Shop Safety

Eye protection, safe lifting, correct tool use, clean floors, ventilation, and following procedures around vehicles and chemicals matter.

7 / 15
🎓

Training Paths

High-school technical programs, career/technical schools, community colleges, apprenticeships or employer training can build skills. Certifications may help demonstrate knowledge.

8 / 15
🔩

Tools

Hand tools • torque wrench • lift • jack stands • scan tool • multimeter • tire equipment.

9 / 15
🗣️

Customer Service

Technicians and service staff need to explain problems without confusing the customer and avoid promising work they cannot confirm.

10 / 15
💬

Workplace Scenario

A customer says the car makes a noise only sometimes. Ask useful questions, document the concern, inspect systematically, and avoid guessing.

11 / 15
✅

Quality Control

After a repair, technicians verify their work, check for leaks or loose parts, follow specifications, and document what was done.

12 / 15
📈

Moving Up

Experience can lead toward advanced diagnostics, diesel, specialty systems, shop leadership, service advising, or business ownership.

13 / 15
🤔

Is This Career for You?

Do you enjoy fixing things, working with your hands, learning technology, and solving problems step by step?

14 / 15
🧠

Career Check

What is the difference between diagnosing a problem and guessing? Name three shop-safety habits.
