What childcare professionals do

Supervise children, support routines, prepare developmentally appropriate activities, communicate with families and coworkers, maintain safe environments, and help children build social, language, motor, and learning skills.

Careers to explore

Childcare worker • preschool teacher • teacher assistant • infant/toddler educator • school-age care worker • nanny • family childcare provider • center director/administrator • early-childhood teacher.

Skills to practice now

Active supervision, patience, communication, safety awareness, following routines, teamwork, professionalism, confidentiality, sanitation, activity planning, and responding calmly to behavior.

Tennessee pathway

Tennessee’s Early Childhood Education Careers program of study is designed for students interested in early-childhood teaching. TECTA also offers orientation, credential support, and education pathways. Requirements vary by role and setting, so students should verify current requirements before employment.

Career Exploration Questions

  1. Which age group would you most like to work with: infants, toddlers, preschool, or school-age children?
  2. How comfortable are you with noise, messes, routines, and repeated directions?
  3. What would be hardest for you about supervising several children at once?
  4. What activity would you enjoy planning for young children?
  5. How would you respond if a child became upset?
  6. Which childcare career would you research next?
