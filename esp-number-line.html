A LITTLE HELP

Classroom controls

One shared screen. No student names, accounts, timers, or scores.

If full screen is unavailable, use focus view. On iPhone, Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen can hide the browser bars.

How to play & keyboard help

Read the equation with a missing answer. Optional one-step hops help children count forward for addition or backward for subtraction. Choose A, B, or C. A correct answer glows, fills the blank, and replays the hops. Tap Next friend to pass the turn.

Keyboard: Tab to move; Enter/Space to activate. A/B/C choose answers, H makes one hop, R reads the question, N goes to the next friend when ready. No dragging is required.

32 turns lets 16 children have two turns. Continue afterward if needed. Each level has at least 45 different equations. No equation repeats in that level until its deck is used up. Switching levels keeps each deck’s place. Anonymous progress lasts in this browser tab until it is closed or a new class starts.
