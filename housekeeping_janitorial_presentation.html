Housekeeping & Janitorial Careers

Clean, safe spaces do not happen by accident. These workers are essential to homes, hotels, schools, hospitals, offices, and businesses.

2/15
🎯

Career Options

Housekeeper, room attendant, custodian, janitor, environmental-services worker, floor-care worker, laundry attendant, and team lead.

3/15
⭐

Daily Tasks

Trash, dusting, surfaces, restrooms, floors, restocking, laundry/linens in some roles, inspections, and reporting maintenance concerns.

4/15
📚

Cleaning vs Disinfecting

Cleaning removes dirt and debris; disinfecting uses approved products/procedures to reduce germs on appropriate surfaces. Follow workplace instructions.

5/15
💡

Chemical Safety

Read labels and safety information, use required PPE, never mix chemicals unless specifically instructed by approved procedures, and store products correctly.

6/15
🛠️

Equipment

Mops, vacuums, carts, microfiber tools, floor equipment with training, PPE, signs, and approved cleaning products.

7/15
🎯

Room/System Routine

Work from an organized checklist so important areas are not missed and clean tools do not contaminate already-clean areas.

8/15
⭐

Safety

Wet-floor signs, sharps/biohazard procedures where applicable, lifting, electrical awareness, ventilation, and reporting hazards.

9/15
📚

Workplace Scenario

You find an unknown spill. Do not touch or mix products blindly. Secure the area and follow the workplace's spill/reporting procedure.

10/15
💡

Privacy & Respect

Workers may enter guest rooms, offices, classrooms, or patient spaces. Respect belongings, privacy, confidentiality, and access rules.

11/15
🛠️

Quality Check

Before leaving: inspect the area, restock supplies, remove waste correctly, return equipment, and document/report issues.

12/15
🎯

Professional Skills

Dependability, detail, time management, honesty, safety awareness, teamwork, and working independently.

13/15
⭐

Moving Up

Housekeeper/custodian → lead → supervisor → facilities/environmental-services management or specialized floor/equipment roles.

14/15
📚

Try It

Create a 10-step cleaning checklist for a classroom or pretend hotel room. Put the steps in a safe, efficient order and perform a final inspection.
